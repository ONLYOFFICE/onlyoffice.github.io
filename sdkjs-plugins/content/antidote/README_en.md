[Lisez-moi](./README.md)

Integrate the writing assistance software Antidote with OnlyOffice to correct your text and to consult the dictionaries and guides without leaving the app.

⚠️ You must have access to Antidote to use its tools. They are not included with this connector.

### HOW IT WORKS
When you add the Antidote Connector, an Antidote button appears in the menu bar. Click it to open the task pane that has three options: Corrector, Dictionaries and Guides.

### 📝 Review and correct your text:
* Click the Corrector button to run your text through Antidote’s corrector.
* Edit the text in Antidote; changes are applied directly to the original document.

### 📚 Consult the dictionaries and language guides:
* Select one or multiple words in your document and click Dictionaries or Guides from the task pane to launch a search for the selection in Antidote.
* Replace a word directly in the original document from a dictionary entry.
***
### Your trust is important to us!
Depending on the Antidote product in use, the connector may transmit your text to Druide’s servers. Rest assured that your text is never used to train large language models without your consent.

Read our [privacy policy](https://www.antidote.info/en/privacy-policy) to learn more about how we handle your personal data.
