## Overview

Asyntai AI Assistant brings your business knowledge base into ONLYOFFICE. Ask a question in the side panel and get an answer from the same knowledge base that powers your [Asyntai](https://asyntai.com) website chatbot: your website pages, help articles, product details and the documents you added. Insert the answer into your text, slide or spreadsheet with one click.

The panel also works in the other direction. Add the text you selected, or the whole text document, to the knowledge base, so your chatbot and your team answer from the newest version of your policies, price lists and guides.

The plugin works in the text, presentation, spreadsheet and PDF editors, in ONLYOFFICE Docs and in ONLYOFFICE Desktop Editors.

## How to use

1. Open the **Plugins** tab and click **Asyntai AI Assistant**.
2. Paste your Asyntai API key and click **Connect**. The key stays in this browser or desktop app.
3. Type a question, or select text in the document and click **Use selected text**. Click **Ask**.
4. Click **Insert into document** to put the answer at the cursor, or **Copy** to copy it.
5. To add content to the knowledge base, enter a title, choose **Selected text** or **Whole document**, and click **Add to knowledge base**.

If your account has more than one website, choose the knowledge base at the top of the panel. Click **Start a new conversation** to ask about a new topic.

## Configuration

1. Sign in to [Asyntai](https://asyntai.com), or [create an account](https://asyntai.com/register/).
2. Open [Settings > API](https://asyntai.com/settings/api/) and copy your API key. The API is part of the Starter plan and higher.
3. Paste the key into the plugin and click **Connect**.

## How to install

Install the plugin from the ONLYOFFICE Plugin Manager. Detailed instructions can be found in the [ONLYOFFICE API documentation](https://api.onlyoffice.com/docs/plugin-and-macros/tutorials/installing/onlyoffice-docs-on-premises/).
