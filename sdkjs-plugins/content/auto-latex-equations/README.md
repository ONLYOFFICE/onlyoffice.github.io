# Auto-LaTeX Equations

Turn LaTeX written in your document text into real math equations.

Type or paste your formulas between dollar signs, for example `$E = mc^2$` or `$$\frac{a}{b}$$`, and the plugin converts them into editable ONLYOFFICE equations.

## How to use

1. Open the **Plugins** tab and click **Auto-LaTeX Equations**. The panel opens on the right.
2. Click **Convert all equations** to convert every `$...$` and `$$...$$` in the document.
3. Optional: check **Automatic conversion on paste**. While the panel is open, LaTeX that you paste (or type) is converted automatically.

## Good to know

- `$...$` and `$$...$$` are both converted to inline equations.
- Plain amounts such as `5$ and 6$` are left untouched: there must be no space after the opening `$` and none before the closing `$`, and no digit right after the closing `$`.
- Automatic conversion works only while the panel is open.
- Works in the document editor (ONLYOFFICE 8.2 or later).

## Languages

English, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese.
