# Change Log

## 1.0.0

* Initial release.
* `BIQUOTE(symbol, [field])` custom function for live forex, metal, crypto, stock, index and commodity prices.
* `BIQUOTE_CONVERT(amount, from, to, [market])` for currency, metal, crypto and Grand Bazaar (Kapalıçarşı) gold conversion.
* Turkish product names (gram altın, çeyrek, yarım, tam…) for Istanbul Grand Bazaar prices.
* Side panel with symbol search, live prices, formula builder and refresh.
* Light and dark theme support; English and Turkish.
