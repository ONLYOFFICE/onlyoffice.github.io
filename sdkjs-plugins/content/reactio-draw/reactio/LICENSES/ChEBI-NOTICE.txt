ChEBI (Chemical Entities of Biological Interest), EMBL-EBI
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/

The 2D drawings in names/depictions.txt (atom places, bonds and wedges of 83
natural products and drugs) are taken from the ChEBI molfiles of those
entries, unchanged except that their coordinates are rounded to 0.01. Each
line names its ChEBI entry (CHEBI:<id>).
